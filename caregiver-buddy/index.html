Privacy Policy for Caregiver Buddy

Effective Date: February 25, 2026

Caregiver Buddy respects your privacy. This app is designed to help caregivers organize important information in a simple, private, and secure way.

Information Collection and Use

Caregiver Buddy does not collect, store, or transmit personal data to any external servers. All information entered into the app is stored locally on your device and remains under your control.

The app does not use analytics, tracking technologies, or advertising identifiers.

In‑App Purchases

Caregiver Buddy offers optional in‑app purchases to unlock premium features. Payments are processed securely by Apple or Google through their respective app stores. Caregiver Buddy does not receive or store payment information.

Third‑Party Services

Caregiver Buddy uses RevenueCat to manage in‑app purchase entitlements. RevenueCat may process anonymous purchase‑related information as required to provide this service. No personal caregiver or care recipient data is shared.

Data Security

Because all care‑related information is stored locally on your device, its security depends on your device’s operating system protections. Caregiver Buddy does not have access to your stored information.

Children’s Privacy

Caregiver Buddy does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app is intended for adult caregivers.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
