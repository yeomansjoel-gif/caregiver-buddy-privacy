Privacy Policy for Caregiver Buddy

Effective Date: February 25, 2026

Caregiver Buddy respects your privacy. This app is designed to help caregivers organize important information in a simple, private, and secure way.

Information Collection and Use

Caregiver Buddy does not collect, store, or transmit personal data to any external servers. All information entered into the app is stored locally on your device and remains under your control.

Caregiver Buddy offers optional in‑app purchases. Payments are processed securely by Apple or Google. Caregiver Buddy does not receive or store payment information.

Third‑Party Services
